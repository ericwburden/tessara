# UAT-7B-01 — Lifecycle observation

As Component manager, record the referenced version identity and revision,
deactivate it, and capture its audit entry. As scoped Dashboard editor, load the
editor and verify a lifecycle finding appears while the saved reference remains
unchanged and rendering is unavailable. Reactivate the version and reload.
Verify the revision advances again, fresh health closes the finding, and no
manual resolve action exists. Restore active health and retain API/UI evidence.
