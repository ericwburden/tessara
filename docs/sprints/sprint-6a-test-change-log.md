# Sprint 6A Test Change Log

This log records changes to pre-existing test expectations and accepted fixtures during Sprint 6A. New additive tests do not require an entry unless they replace or weaken earlier proof. A blank rationale blocks closeout.

## Rules

- Production regressions are fixed in production code; a failing test is not authorization to edit its expectation.
- Every changed/deleted/skipped/loosened assertion or fixture must cite an approved requirement/decision and identify equivalent or stronger replacement proof.
- No accepted v1 fixture may be rewritten after contract acceptance. Pre-acceptance fixture corrections remain recorded here so reviewers can see why bytes/digests changed.
- Contract acceptance is the reviewed commit that completes Sprint 6A plan step 2 with every canonical/invalid fixture and expected-digest test green; closeout records that commit explicitly.
- Unexpected ignored, skipped, filtered, retry-only, or timeout-dependent results fail closeout.
